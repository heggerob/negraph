Negraph Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Negraph is a parody app made by Robin Hegge. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use it. The short version: the app does not collect any data about you.

No data collected

Negraph has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

What stays on your device

A few settings, such as whether sound is on, are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and they are removed when you delete the app.

The slider

The color you pick on the chart is chosen by you. The app does not use the camera, does not look at photos and does not try to detect anything about you. Your choice is not saved or sent anywhere.

The embedded video

The start screen can show the original comedy sketch through YouTube, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The video is loaded from YouTube, so YouTube (Google) may receive technical information such as your IP address, as with any web page. This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Negraph does not receive any of this information.

Children

Negraph does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
